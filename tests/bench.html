SYNTH CPU bench

Renders each scenario through the real engine and reports its CPU load (fraction of real time; 1 = the whole audio deadline). The Serum and FX rows have iPad targets; every row must stay under 0.6 on an iPad Pro.

On the iPad
  1. Plug the iPad in, close the SYNTH Canvas app and other apps.
  2. Open this page in Safari and tap Run 5 passes. Keep the page in front until it finishes (the page asks the screen to stay on; a few minutes).
  3. Tap Copy results and paste them into the chat (or Share… to send them to the Mac).
