Renders every audible track (mute/solo respected) offline — no clicks or dropouts regardless of CPU.

How it works. Every sound is generated in real time by a custom DSP engine running on the browser’s audio thread — no samples. Oscillators, filters, envelopes and effects are modelled from their circuits and computed in 64-bit floating point at up to 8× the output sample rate (at most 384 kHz internally), then band-limited back down with ~100 dB Kaiser half-band filters.
