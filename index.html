Play: computer keys A–; (white) and W E T Y U O P (black), Z/X octave, Space sustain. Click or touch the keyboard (lower on a key = louder), or plug in a MIDI controller (Chrome, Edge, Firefox). Controls: drag up/down, Shift-drag for fine, scroll wheel, double-click to reset.

How it works. Every sound is generated in real time by a custom DSP engine running on the browser’s audio thread — no samples. Oscillators, filters, envelopes and effects are modelled from their circuits and computed in 64-bit floating point at up to 8× the output sample rate, then band-limited back down with ~100 dB Kaiser half-band filters.
